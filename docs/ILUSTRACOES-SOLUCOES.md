# Ilustrações conceituais das soluções

Geradas com o gerador integrado OpenAI em 7 de outubro de 2026. São imagens editoriais conceituais para os cards da página `/solucoes`, não fotografias de obras, projetos executivos, especificações ou instruções de engenharia. As fotos reais do portfólio e das demais áreas do site permanecem separadas.

## Direção visual compartilhada

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: 4:3 landscape editorial illustration for a premium sports-court service card. Style/medium: polished architectural 3D isometric illustration, clearly a conceptual render and not photography; refined miniature maquette, precise clean edges, subtle contact shadows, restrained tactile materials. Scene/backdrop: simple warm pale sage background (#EAF1ED), no room or horizon line. Composition: one centered, complete subject with generous breathing room, viewed from a consistent elevated 30-degree isometric angle; readable when cropped to a 4:3 card. Color palette: forest green, deep court blue, white, warm sand, and muted stone gray, cohesive across a family of illustrations. Constraints: no words, letters, numbers, logos, people, photorealism, signage, watermark, border, UI, extra decorative objects, or technical dimensions.
```

## Prompts de cena

1. **Construção de quadras** — A newly planned outdoor multi-sport court as a crisp miniature architectural model: deep blue playing area, green safety perimeter, simple accurate white sport markings, neat low fence and discreet light poles. Show the full court and its surrounding base as one coherent new-build concept.
2. **Reforma de quadras** — A conceptual court-renewal scene on a single court platform: one small surface section is lifted to reveal a clean layered base, while the remaining playing surface is freshly refinished in blue and green with white markings. Keep it elegant and orderly, not a literal before-and-after and not a real project.
3. **Pisos esportivos** — A refined material-selection still life of three miniature sports-floor swatches arranged on a low plinth: smooth blue resilient finish, warm orange indoor court coating, and neutral gray concrete/asphalt surface, each with a small white line detail. Focus on texture and material comparison, no labels.
4. **Grama sintética** — A compact small-sided soccer playing surface with vivid but natural forest-green artificial grass, visibly short soft turf fibers at the front edge, crisp white boundary markings and two modest goals. Show enough surrounding base to read as a miniature sports field.
5. **Campos de futebol** — A complete small football field in deep natural green turf, with clearly legible white touchlines, halfway line, center circle, penalty areas and two simple goals. Add a restrained green perimeter and a neat cutaway edge revealing the prepared base, no stands.
6. **Quadras poliesportivas** — A single blue-and-green multi-sport court with carefully organized white and pale-yellow overlapping basketball and futsal markings, one neat basketball hoop and understated net posts. The full playing area is visible, readable and uncluttered.
7. **Quadras de tênis** — A complete classic tennis court in rich deep blue with a forest-green perimeter, crisp white singles and doubles lines, centered taut net and two understated posts. Show a clean low court platform with no players, grandstands or extra equipment.
8. **Quadras de beach tennis** — A compact beach-tennis court filled with softly textured pale warm sand, clean blue boundary lines and a centered dark net with slim posts. Add a restrained low sand-retaining edge, surrounded only by the pale sage backdrop; no beach, ocean or people.
9. **Pistas de atletismo** — A complete oval running track with four clear terracotta-red lanes and fine white lane markings around a simple green infield. Show a clean isometric miniature platform, even curves and generous neutral backdrop, no runners or stadium.
10. **Estruturas e acessórios** — A curated isometric arrangement of sports-court infrastructure on one low plinth: a short dark-green chain-link fence section with gate, a compact basketball hoop, a futsal goal frame, and a pair of net posts with a taut net. Keep each item distinct, balanced and realistically proportioned; no court, people or extra props.
11. **Playgrounds** — A welcoming small playground on a square muted-green safety surface: one compact timber-and-green climbing structure with a short slide and two swings, clear circulation around it, a low curb. Friendly, premium and safe-looking, with no children, no signage and no extra toys.
12. **Paisagismo** — A small sports-and-leisure landscape concept viewed isometrically: a clean pale-stone walking path curves through green planting beds, a few varied trees and shrubs, and one simple bench beside the outer edge of a blue sports court. Balanced planting, clear circulation, restrained and uncluttered.
